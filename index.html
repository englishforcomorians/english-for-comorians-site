About this site

English For Comorians gives you free lessons from Pre-A1 all the way to C2. Every lesson comes with a description you can read right away, and a "View lesson" button that opens whatever's attached to it — this could be a video, an audio, a PDF, a chat-style dialog to practice, or a mix of these. Not every lesson has all of them, but every lesson has something to help you learn.
